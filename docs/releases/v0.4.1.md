Kairo 0.4.1 fixes three things people saw in 0.4.0's new screens.

### Fixed

- **The menu bar and tray no longer say Connected before there is a subscription.** With no subscription, the first line says **Not Set Up** (未设置), as Home does, with no tick and a dim icon. It says Connected once a subscription is added and Kairo is connected.
- **Apps with no logo of their own show one letter.** MiniMax, Yuanbao, Ernie, Zhipu and other apps without a picture used to squeeze their whole name into the small square, a few letters per line. They now show their first letter.
- **"Direct" everywhere, never DIRECT.** When your subscription sends a kind straight out (for instance its Games group is set to DIRECT), the kind's card on **Apps** said DIRECT, while the menus and the tray said Direct (直连). It now says **Direct** (直连), and a blocked kind says **Block** (拦截), in both languages.

### Updating

Kairo 0.1.2 and later on macOS and Linux: click the download icon at the bottom left, then **Restart to Update**. Windows and Android: the same download icon (on Android it sits on Home).

Coming from 0.3.x: your exits and choices move to the new Apps page once, as described in the [0.4.0 notes](https://github.com/daoleno/kairo/releases/tag/v0.4.0).
