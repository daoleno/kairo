Kairo 0.4.0 speaks Simplified Chinese, and sorts what you use into apps: every app has a kind, every kind has its own exit, and one page, **Apps**, holds them all. It takes the place of Exits and Apps & Sites.

### What's new

- **Kairo in Simplified Chinese.** Every window, menu, tray item and message is in English and 简体中文. **Settings › Language**: System (the default), English or 简体中文. A change applies at once, menu bar and tray included, with no restart.
- **Apps by kind.** An app is a service you use (Netflix, Claude, Steam, Hugging Face), known by its sites; the programs that use it show under it. Each app has a kind, and each kind has an exit: **AI**, **Streaming**, **Games**, **Downloads**, **Dev tools** and **Everything else**, each with a country, a server and a word for how it is doing.
  - **AI** stays pinned to its server, as before. **Everything else** is the fastest place unless you pick one. **Downloads** follows the fastest place. **Streaming**, **Games** and **Dev tools** go the way your subscription routes them until you pick a place.
  - One app can have an exit of its own: its kind's, Everything else, the subscription's way, Direct, a country, or Block.
- **The Apps page.** The six kinds with their exits on the left. Pick a kind's country, then a server. Below are the apps used this week, by kind, and the sites Kairo saw but could not sort (one click makes one an app). Click an app to open its sheet: its exit, the programs that use it and its week.
- **A check for each kind.** Every ten minutes Kairo checks what matters for each kind: AI, whether it answers and the country it sees; Streaming, which catalog the service shows and Mbit/s; Games, connection time, jitter and loss; Downloads, MB/s; Dev tools, HTTPS and SSH. When something is wrong, the row offers one button to fix it.
- **Home shows the apps you use.** Below the AI table there is a card for each kind used in the last 7 days, with up to three apps each.
- **Activity by app.** Each program lists the apps it talks to, coloured by kind. A row's menu sets **Exit for This Program** or **Add as App**.
- **Menu bar and tray.** One submenu for each kind's exit.
- **Kairo on a machine with no screen.** `kairo-server` runs Kairo on a Linux machine with no screen, and its window is a page in the browser, behind a password. See the README's Install section and [SECURITY-NOTES](https://github.com/daoleno/kairo/blob/main/crates/kairo-server/SECURITY-NOTES.md) before opening it to other devices.

### What moved

- **Exits** and **Apps & Sites** are now **Apps**. The sidebar has four pages: Home, Apps, Activity and Subscriptions (⌘1 to ⌘4, Ctrl on Linux and Windows), with Settings below them.
- **Settings › AI** is gone. "AI tools use one exit" is now **Settings › Advanced › Use the subscription's rules only**, the other way round: turned on, only the apps you gave an exit of their own keep it.
- The Downloads exit and its mirror switch are on the Downloads kind, in **Apps**.

### After updating

- **Your choices carry over, once.** The first start of 0.4.0 moves your settings to the new form, and nothing changes where your traffic goes:
  - the AI exit (pinned or following the place) becomes the AI kind's exit, and the Downloads exit, Fastest and the mirror move to the Downloads kind;
  - every app and site you added on Apps & Sites keeps its exit: as that app, when Kairo knows it, otherwise as an app of your own under Everything else;
  - "AI tools use one exit" turned off becomes **Use the subscription's rules only** turned on;
  - if you had sent Kimi, DeepSeek and the other domestic AI through the proxy, they stay on the subscription's way.
- **Going back to 0.3.x** after this is not supported: 0.3.2 does not read the new settings, and its AI exit and Apps & Sites start empty.
- Streaming, Games and Dev tools have no place of their own yet: they keep going the way your subscription sends them until you pick one in **Apps**.

### Updating

Kairo 0.1.2 and later on macOS and Linux: click the download icon at the bottom left, then **Restart to Update**. Windows and Android: the same download icon (on Android it sits on Home).

Android: arm64 phones with Android 9 or later only. Kairo is signed with its own key, the same one for every release (certificate SHA-256 `95d8456a40eac46549f4dba6f6a26181612e724c229a397f2a46515de67c80f6`).
