Kairo 0.4.3 keeps Android on when you leave it, and puts a switch for it in Quick Settings.

### What's new

- **Kairo stays on when you leave it.** Swiping Kairo away no longer matters, and if Android stops it to free memory it comes back by itself within seconds. After a restart of the phone or an update, Kairo is on again if it was on, without opening the app. Android's **Always-on VPN** (Settings → Network → VPN → Kairo) works too.
- **A Quick Settings tile.** Turn Kairo on and off from the pull-down without opening the app: it shows On, Off or Connecting…, follows the switch in the app, and asks to unlock on the lock screen. Long-press it to open Kairo. On Android 13+ add it from Settings → **Quick Settings · Add**.

### Fixed

- When Kairo's core could not start, or stopped again and again, the VPN could stay up with nothing behind it and the phone had no network. Now the VPN goes with the core.
- Apps' server picker: a press on a server is never lost while readings change.

### Updating

Kairo 0.1.2 and later on macOS and Linux: click the download icon at the bottom left, then **Restart to Update**. Windows and Android: the same download icon (on Android it sits on Home).

Coming from 0.3.x: your exits and choices move to the new Apps page once, as described in the [0.4.0 notes](https://github.com/daoleno/kairo/releases/tag/v0.4.0).

Android: some phones (Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo, Huawei) stop apps in the background unless you allow it. If Kairo turns off after you swipe it away, allow **Autostart** / **background activity** for Kairo and lock it in Recents.
