A new window, with numbers: five pages in a sidebar, and Kairo now keeps statistics on your computer, so you can see how much went where, how each AI service held up, and whether your data lasts. Kairo 0.1.2 and later update themselves.

### New

- **Five pages.** **Home**, **Exits**, **Activity**, **Apps & Sites** and **Subscriptions** in a sidebar, **Settings** below them. ⌘1 to ⌘5 (Ctrl on Linux) open the five pages, ⌘, opens Settings, ⌘K searches everything.
- **First run.** With no subscription, Home asks for one and nothing else. A provider's own message (a blocked fetch, an expired plan) is shown in its words.
- **Home.** The connect switch with the mode (Smart, Everything, Direct) and how apps follow Kairo (System settings or Every app); the three exits, each with a country and how it is doing; one row per AI service with what Kairo measured. A small line on each exit shows the last day.
- **Exits.** AI, Everything else and Downloads each get a country: pick a country, then a server, or **Fastest**. A server's delay is a word (Good, Slow, Timeout) with the number beside it.
- **Statistics.** Recorded on this computer only, per hour, kept 30 days (`stats.json` in Kairo's data folder; **Clear History** in Activity removes it). Nothing is sent anywhere. Kairo records with the window closed.
  - **Activity** shows traffic per hour stacked by the exit it left through, the top apps, each AI service's share, and a reachability row per service. Today, 7 days or 30 days. Move over a bar for exact values.
  - **An AI service** opens its week: reachable for every hour, how fast the API answered, which country it saw, and how its 90-second hold went.
  - **Exits › Quality**: a test of the server each exit is on every ten minutes (response time, failed tests) and a log of every switch, yours or Kairo's.
  - **Subscriptions** shows use so far with the recent pace, and whether the data lasts until the plan ends.
  - Until there is an hour of data, the pages say "Collecting data".
- **A Download button, bottom left.** Kairo checks for a new version on its own; when there is one, **Download** appears under the sidebar. Click it, then **Restart to Update**. Nothing happens without a click.
- **Menu bar and tray.** The switch, the AI exit and Everything else as submenus by country (with Fastest), the mode, and Settings.

### Fixed

- **Use Every App** (TUN) now starts the core when it was not running, and the banner for an AI tool that goes around Kairo says what to do.
- **Copy for terminal** copies an `export` line with `NO_PROXY`.
- Pressing the connect switch twice soon after the first press undoes it (the last press wins), instead of being ignored.
- On macOS 27, Kairo's own connections stay out of Activity, and a short request is named by `lsof` while it still exists.
- macOS: the login item is tied to the app and named **Open at login**, as macOS calls it. The Taiwan flag is drawn where the system font has none.
- **Downloads** has a mirror card: Kairo tests the package mirror and says in words whether it is faster than going straight.

### Changed

- **Removed** from the main surfaces: YAML editing of a config, the speed graph, per-server delay numbers on Home, the TUN stack and the core's log level. They were not used; the core's own config is still readable under Settings › Advanced.
- Groups, Rules, Logs and DNS moved into **Apps & Sites** and **Settings › Advanced**, a search away with ⌘K.

### Good to know

- The AI checks and the exit tests make a few small requests through the exits every ten minutes; Settings › AI turns the AI part off.
- Statistics from before this version do not exist; the pages start empty and fill in.

### Updating

Kairo 0.1.2 and later download this release in the background. Click **Restart to Update** in the sidebar or under Settings › About. Versions before 0.1.2 do not update themselves: install 0.3.0 by hand.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the checksums of every file. Every package includes the mihomo core v1.19.33.

**macOS, first open** (new installs only): the app is ad hoc signed, not notarized, so macOS stops it the
first time. Open Kairo once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway** next
to Kairo. Or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Kairo.app` before opening it.
