Kairo 0.3.2 makes the lists easier to read and easier to clean up: apps and sites show their real icons, what you add can be removed, and a lot of small explaining text is gone.

### What's new

- **Real icons.** Apps and sites show their own icon instead of a letter: the icon of the installed app, the site's own icon (fetched in the background, never slowing a page), or the logo of a well-known service. Apps also show their proper name ("Google Chrome", "Visual Studio Code") on Apps & Sites and on Activity.
- **Remove what you added.** Every app and site you added has its own **×** button on Apps & Sites.
- **A closable update-failure card.** When a subscription can't update, the card says so once, with **Retry** and a close button. Closed, it stays away until the next update fails.
- **Groups from the subscription.** The subscription in use has a **Groups** button. It opens the groups, the sidebar stays on Subscriptions, and Back returns there.
- **Update is an icon.** One download icon at the bottom left of the window (no text): a ring fills while it downloads, a disc means it is ready. Nothing shows when there is no update.
- **Fewer captions.** The lines under rows, pages and dialogs that only explained themselves are gone. **Add App** and **Add Site** no longer end in "…".

### Fixed

- A Chrome web app (for instance Linear) could give "chrome" its own icon instead of the browser's.
- A failed update no longer also pops up a red message that says the same thing as the card.

### Updating

Kairo 0.1.2 and later on macOS and Linux: click the download icon at the bottom left, then **Restart to Update**. Windows and Android: the same download icon (on Android it sits on Home).
