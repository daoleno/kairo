Kairo 0.4.2 fixes the layout of Subscriptions and Apps on phones.

### Fixed

- **A subscription that could not update says why across the whole width.** On a phone the provider's message sat in a narrow column beside Retry, one or two characters a line. The card now reads its name and "couldn't update" first, then the message across the card, then **Retry** and close. A long message stops after three lines with **Show All** (展开).
- **Apps fills the width of a phone.** The kinds' cards are in two equal columns across the screen (they filled two thirds of it), **All apps** is a filter above them rather than a card among them, and every kind shows its whole name (流媒体, 开发工具, 其他流量 and AI, which showed none). Each app's check (地区：日本, Sees Japan) is under its name, read whole.
- **Back on Android** leaves Settings for Home and closes an app's sheet, instead of leaving Kairo.
- **Add an app** says 程序 / 网站 in Chinese (it said A program / A site).
- On a desktop window made narrow, a subscription's buttons go under its name and numbers instead of squeezing them.

### Updating

Kairo 0.1.2 and later on macOS and Linux: click the download icon at the bottom left, then **Restart to Update**. Windows and Android: the same download icon (on Android it sits on Home).

Coming from 0.3.x: your exits and choices move to the new Apps page once, as described in the [0.4.0 notes](https://github.com/daoleno/kairo/releases/tag/v0.4.0).
